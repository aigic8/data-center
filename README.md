# Data Center

This my bachelor's research project on modeling data center energy demand and
heat generation, and the surrounding energy systems that could supply them. The
repository is organized into three main areas.

## Repository Structure

```
data-center/
├── demand/            # Data center demand modeling
│   ├── data_demand/           # Power demand models for CPU, GPU and DC
│   └── probability_demand/    # Probabilistic demand modeling (WIP)
├── energy-system/     # Energy supply side modeling
│   └── apps/energy-system-modeling/  # PV and wind turbine models
└── heat-generation/   # Server heat generation models
```

## Demand

### Data Demand (`demand/data_demand`)

Models the power demand of a data center based on real workload traces (MIT GPU
data, Alibaba 2020 cluster trace). The work is split into numbered notebooks
under `src/`:

1. Merging raw dataframes (`pods.csv`, `nodes.csv`)
2. GPU active power modeling
3. GPU idle power modeling
4. CPU active power modeling
5. CPU idle power modeling
6. Total data center power modeling

Resulting plots are in `plots/`, and the collection of related papers is in
`papers/` (CPU models, DC power surveys, GPU data center characteristics). See
`demand/data_demand/README.md` for the research todo list and `docs/modeling.md`
for a literature overview.

### Probability Demand (`demand/probability_demand`) (depreciation)

Demand Generation based on probability and distribution of the source data.

## Energy System (`energy-system/apps/energy-system-modeling`)

Modeling of energy supply systems for the data center, implemented in `lib/`
with supporting notebooks in `notebooks/`:

- `lib/pv.py`: photovoltaic system modeling (panel and inverter selection,
  system design)
- `lib/wt.py`: wind turbine modeling, including wind speed change of height via
  the power law

Referenced design sources for PV panels (e.g. SunPower, Trina, Longi) and
inverters (SMA Sunny Tripower) are documented in
`apps/energy-system-modeling/docs/README.md`.

## Heat Generation (`heat-generation`)

Models the heat generated by servers:

- **Static model**: constant heat generation, independent of server utilization
- **Dynamic model**: heat generation based on CPU/GPU utilization

The dynamic model is based on
[Energy and Buildings (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2014.10.058),
where GPU and CPU heat generation is sufficient and other components can be
neglected. A utilization based lab script is in `lab/utilization_server_cpu.py`.

## Requirements

- Python 3.11+
- Jupyter (notebooks are the primary working format)

## Status

This is an ongoing research project. Current focus areas include utilization
based server power models, data center reliability analysis, and workload driven
demand modeling.
